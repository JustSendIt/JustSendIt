Send Calls & the Full Rulebook 📜

The headline game — and every rule behind it, in plain English. These are the exact numbers the server uses. It's a game on public on‑chain data, not financial advice.

📣 What's a Send Call?

Call a token you believe in and it posts a permanent, live scorecard to your wall — a public, timestamped claim that it's going to run. Once posted, a call can never be edited or deleted, and its all‑time high is kept forever, even if the token later dies. Performance is measured in Xs, where +100% = 1x (so +340% shows as +3.4x). Every call carries a live grade from E → S (S = a legendary 20x+ run) based on its peak, and the token needs ≥ $500 liquidity to be callable at all — no farming dust pools.

📣 Call = +120× Send size (up to ×100)× Holder Boost

Send size: every $100 of the token you actually buy and hold = ×1 Send Power on the call (cap ×100). It's read on‑chain as min(bought, still‑held) × price so it's hard to fake, and it's locked in at call time. As your call runs, each whole X pays 170 × that X (1x → +170 … up to 50x → +8,500), and while it stays in profit you earn a compounding 💎 diamond‑hands hold bonus that grows the longer and higher it holds — and faster still when the people who Sent It on your call are in profit too. Every call has one lifetime budget — the Send Power it takes to reach Level 70 (≈737,627): the opening award may take at most a tenth of it, the ladder at most three tenths, and at least 60% is reserved for that hold bonus. Holding in profit is the main event, not the ladder — and Level 100 is about twenty perfect calls, so no single call carries anyone to the top.

🚀 "Send It!" & the senders listFollow a call · +30 on a verified buy · same hold bonus ▾

Tap 🚀 Send It! to Send It on someone else's call: +30 (scaled by your own Send size and Holder Boost), paid on your first Send per call, capped at 30/day — you can't Send It on your own call. Your Xs count from the price when you Sent It, and you earn the same diamond‑hands hold bonus the caller does. Each call shows who's behind it, ranked by conviction — $ put in × how long held × how many Xs up — and anyone who has sold out drops below every current holder. The card shows the top 3; expand for everyone.

🚦 Daily limits & fair playEarn calls · RUGGED · DYOR ▾

Everyone gets 5 calls a day (rolling 24h). It floats on your quality: a matured call that doubled (≥1x) earns you +1 (cap 5); an all‑duds day costs −1 (floor 1). Your Diamond level multiplies the whole allowance by 2^level (up to ×1024). Two guardrails keep it honest:

  • 💀 RUGGED: if a token you called has its liquidity pulled (pool drops below $100), the call is permanently marked RUGGED and your daily limit is docked −2.
  • ⚠️ No‑DYOR flag: call a token without first opening its full on‑chain detail and the call is stamped as "not researched" so others know.
  • Spam trap: blow your whole day's allowance in under an hour and you land in Read‑Only Mode.
🔇 Read‑Only Mode & redeeming with $SendBuy your way back · then hold ▾

Spamming mutes your account, escalating per offense: 24 hours → 1 week → permanent. While muted you can't post, call, react, vote, follow or track — but you can still browse, show up daily, and buy & hold (your Holder Boost keeps compounding). You can lift any mute early by buying and holding more $Send, verified on‑chain: $25 per 24h of a timed mute ($175 for a week), or a flat $1,000 for a permanent one. Only $Send you buy after your baseline counts — a pre‑existing bag can't fake it. Then comes a probation hold: keep that $Send for the term (40 days for a permanent buy‑out). Sell during it and a timed mute returns with its timer doubled; a permanent one goes back to permanent.

🛡️ Safety tools — honest by designScanner · health score · Safer tab ▾

Every safety readout is an automated heuristic scan of public on‑chain data — it is not an audit, and it never simulates a buy or sell. A starting point for your own research, never a "buy."

  • Contract scanner: reads a token's verified source and flags owner powers that can trap you — blacklist 🔴, changeable fees, mint, pausable, trading toggle, max limits. Unverified code is itself a red flag.
  • LP‑lock check: reads how much liquidity sits in burn addresses / lockers (≥50% → "looks locked").
  • Health score (0–100): starts at 100 and subtracts a weight per risk (honeypot −45, dumping −32, serial deployer −26, thin liquidity −26, whale ≥30% −20, unverified −15, and more). Any high‑severity flag blocks the green tier.
  • Safer tab (the default) shows only tokens that aren't risky and score ≥ 75; honeypots and serial deployers are always hidden there.
  • Wallet tracker: a browser‑computed PNL report — it never signs or moves funds — and flags "PNL is partial" for very active wallets.

Personal data — your email, which wallets are yours, 2FA secrets, tracked wallets, tracker reports — is stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) under a key that lives outside the database; session cookies are kept only as hashes and passwords only as scrypt hashes, so a stolen database is unreadable on its own. You can add an authenticator app, a wallet signature, or (for wallet accounts that add an email + password) your password as two-factor. Nothing is sold or shared; the site never asks for a seed phrase. Read the details in the project README.
