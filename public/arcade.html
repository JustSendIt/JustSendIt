🎰 How it plays

  1. Pull the lever (tap it, or drag the red ball down) or press Spin.
  2. The reels stop left to right. Only the middle row, on the red payline, counts.
  3. Three of a kind scores, from three cherries up to three 🚀rockets for the jackpot. Any two rockets score, and so do cherries on the left.

📖 How Rocket Run works

  1. Launch. The rocket takes off at 1.00× and the multiplier climbs — slowly at first, then faster and faster.
  2. Cash out whenever you like. Your cash-out settles at the multiplier the server sees when your tap lands — a moment after the one showing — and that becomes your Send Power boost for the next 24 hours.
  3. Or leave it too long. Every flight has a hidden crash point. When the rocket reaches it, it blows and the flight is over, with no boost — that's the whole game.

Nobody knows the crash point — not even this page. It's rolled on the server the moment you launch and stays there until your flight ends, so it can't be read, guessed or pre-computed by your browser. You find out where it was the moment the rocket blows, or when you cash out first.

One flight per day (resets at 00:00 UTC). Your boost is added to the Send Power you earn from posting, reacting and everything else, on top of your Holder, OG and community boosts — boosts add, they don't multiply.

🎮 This is a free bonus game. There is nothing to buy, nothing to put in and nothing to lose — a flight that ends early just means no boost today. Not gambling, not an investment, no money involved. Entertainment only.

📈 The boost curve

The multiplier is e0.06 × seconds — a pure function of how long you stay up. Your boost is 1 + (multiplier − 1) ÷ 4, capped.
