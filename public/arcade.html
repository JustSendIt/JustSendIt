📖 How it works

  1. Launch. The rocket takes off at 1.00× and the multiplier climbs — slowly at first, then faster and faster.
  2. Cash out whenever you like. Whatever the multiplier reads when you tap becomes your Send Power boost for the next 24 hours.
  3. Or leave it too long. Every flight has a hidden crash point. Tap after it, and the flight is over with no boost — that's the whole game.

Nobody knows the crash point — not even this page. It's rolled on the server the moment you launch and stays there until your flight ends, so it can't be read, guessed or pre-computed by your browser. You only find out where it was when you tap.

One flight per day (resets at 00:00 UTC). Your boost multiplies the Send Power you earn from posting, reacting and everything else, and it stacks on your Holder, OG and community multipliers.

🎮 This is a free bonus game. There is nothing to buy, nothing to put in and nothing to lose — a flight that ends early just means no boost today. Not gambling, not an investment, no money involved. Entertainment only.
