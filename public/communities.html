Communities 🏘️

Rally around a token. Paste a contract to start a community — it goes LIVE at 10 members. Anyone can read a live community's public wall, and every community also has a 🔒 holders-only wall that only its verified holders can see. Members (verified holders) post photos, GIFs & videos on both and earn a ⚡ 10× Send Power multiplier on everything. Every community lists its members ranked by member level — and participating levels both you and the community up, exponentially.

📖 How communities & the points work — read this
  • 🏛️ Anyone can start one by pasting a token's contract address (needs ≥ $500 liquidity). A community is not an endorsement — the person who starts it is a community starter, never a "dev".
  • 🔟 A community goes LIVE once 10 real holders opt in. To start, join, or post you must actually hold that token — verified on-chain from a linked (read-only) wallet — plus no more than 2 counting opt-ins per network. Holding is checked on an ongoing basis: sell or move the tokens out and your spot (and the 10×) is revoked.
  • 🛠️ One exception to "you must hold": the token's own deployer/owner wallet (verified on-chain, from a wallet you've signed for) may start that token's community without holding it — devs often keep a clean wallet. That opt-in does not count as one of the 10 verified holders, so a dev can't tip their own community live.
  • 🔒 Two walls. Every live community has a public wall anyone can read, and a holders-only wall only its verified holders can see — refused by the server to everyone else, and never shown on the Send Wall, on a profile, or in the public data feed. Sell the token and the private wall closes again.
  • ⚡ 10× Send Power: while you're in ≥1 live community, a flat 10× boost is added to everything you earn — every post, reaction and comment inside it included. Being in five communities is still one 10×, and it adds on top of your Holder Boost and OG bonus — boosts add, they don't multiply.
  • 👑 Founder bonus: the person whose community reaches 10 members earns a big one-time Send Power bonus.
  • 🏆 Community level (exponential curve) rises as distinct members stay active — posting, reacting, and calling the token. The most active communities float to the top.
  • 💎 Your member level (your “conviction”) is per community — it climbs the longer you're a member and the more you post. It shows as a badge next to that token in the Convicted In section of your public wall. (Both levels are daily-capped so no one can farm them.)

    🏆 This week's competition

    🧮 How the weekly score works
    • ✍️ A community only scores when its members are active inside it — a new member joining, posts, reactions and comments on that community's wall, and Send Calls on its own token by its members.
    • 🧢 Every member's contribution is daily-capped, so one busy account can't farm a community up the board.
    • 💰 Market cap, price and holder count count for nothing here. This board measures activity, full stop.
    • 🗓️ The week runs Monday 00:00 UTC → Monday 00:00 UTC. At the rollover every weekly score resets to zero; all-time community level is untouched.
    • 🎉 It's a leaderboard for fun — entertainment only. There's no prize, no entry, and nothing to buy.
