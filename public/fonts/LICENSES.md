# Font licences

These typefaces are served from this site instead of Google Fonts, so no visitor's browser contacts Google to draw a page. The files are the Latin and Latin Extended subsets Google Fonts publishes, unmodified. They are not covered by this repository's CC0 dedication. Each one keeps its own licence, and the full text sits beside the files.
