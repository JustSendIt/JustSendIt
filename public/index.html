How To Buy

Five steps, about ten minutes. Read the 🔐 seed-phrase step twice.

1

Get a wallet

Robinhood Wallet supports Robinhood Chain out of the box, so you can skip step 2. Get Robinhood Wallet ↗

Any of these work too. Tap one to install it, or connect one you already have:

    ⚠️ Only ever download from the official site or your phone's app store: fake wallet apps are the #1 way people get robbed. Connecting here is read-only: you sign a message to prove the wallet is yours, nothing more. A wallet that can't sign (GMGN, say) can be proved by a burn instead: start 🔥 Burn Verification in the sign-in panel, then send any amount of $SEND or ETH from that wallet to the burn address yourself — never another token, never anywhere else.

    🔐

    Lock down your seed phrase

    Your wallet's 12–24 word seed phrase is your money.

    • Write it on paper, twice. Never a screenshot, email or cloud note.
    • No legit person will ever ask for it. Anyone who does — "support", "admins", "airdrop claims" — is scamming you.
    • Never type it into a website. Your wallet only asks for it when you restore the app.
    • Holding real money? Use a hardware wallet (Ledger, Trezor) and a separate small wallet for trades.
    • Read every wallet pop-up before you sign. An "approval" out of nowhere? Close it.
    2

    Add Robinhood Chain

    Robinhood Chain is an Ethereum L2 built on Arbitrum Orbit. Add it in one click, or by hand:

    Bridge some ETH over

    Got ETH (or anything) on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum or another EVM chain? Bridge it with Relay: it lands as ETH on Robinhood Chain, usually in under a minute.

    No crypto yet? A third-party on-ramp such as MoonPay sells ETH by card (their sign-up, their fees, their rules). Then bridge it here.

    Swap into $SEND

    Track it

    Tap "Add to Wallet" on the $SEND card so it shows in your wallet. Then, with your free account and its $SEND wallet check, track any wallet in the Wallet Tracker: its coins, live value and PNL, private to you.

    Real talk ⚠️ Memecoins are maximum risk: prices can go to zero and liquidity can vanish. $SEND takes 1% on every buy, sell and transfer (it goes to the liquidity pool). Only use money you can afford to lose, check every address on the explorer, and treat every crypto DM as a scam. Entertainment, not financial advice.
