How To Buy — The Easy Way 📖

Never touched crypto before? No stress. Five steps, ten minutes, and you're on Robinhood Chain. Read the 🔐 seed-phrase step twice — wallet security is the whole game.

1

Get an EVM wallet 🦊

Download MetaMask (metamask.io) or Rabby (rabby.io) — browser extension or mobile app. Only ever download from the official site or your phone's app store. Fake wallet apps are the #1 way people get robbed.

🔐

Lock down your seed phrase (do NOT skip this)

When you create the wallet you'll get a 12–24 word seed phrase. That phrase is your money.

  • Write it on paper (twice), store it somewhere safe. Never in a screenshot, email, or cloud note.
  • No legit person will ever ask for it. Anyone who does — "support", "admins", "airdrop claims" — is scamming you.
  • Never type it into a website. Wallets only ask for it when restoring the app itself.
  • Holding a meaningful amount? Use a hardware wallet (Ledger/Trezor) and a separate "hot" wallet with small amounts for degen trades.
  • Before signing anything, read what the wallet popup says. If a site asks for a weird "approval" out of nowhere — close it.
