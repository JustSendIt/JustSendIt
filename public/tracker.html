Wallet Tracker 💼

Track any wallet on Robinhood Chain: holdings, every token transaction, a value-over-time chart, cost basis and PNL — read from the chain by this site, so the explorer never learns which wallets you watch. Read-only: nothing is ever signed or moved.

On the public node a wallet's first read can take an hour or more. You can leave: it keeps its place in line, keeps reading, is saved, and you get a notification when it is ready — or when the node refuses it for so long that it stops; it goes on from where it stopped when you come back. Later refreshes read only what is new and take seconds.

⚠️ Numbers are best-effort from public chain data — not a guarantee, not an audit, not advice. Copying a wallet's trades can lose you money. Do your own research.

What you get 🔍

  • 💼 Track up to 10 wallets — yours, a friend's, or any address you're curious about. Private to your account.
  • 📊 Holdings, portfolio value & a value-over-time chart — every token the wallet holds, priced from live pools on the chain; USD at today's ETH price.
  • 🧾 Every token transaction & cost basis — buys, sells and transfers reconstructed from the chain, whoever sent them, with an average entry price per token.
  • 📈 Realized & unrealized PNL — what's been locked in by selling, and what's still on paper.
  • 💾 Saved history — on the public node a wallet's first read can take an hour or more; you can leave, it keeps reading and is saved to your account, you get a notification when it is ready, and later refreshes read only what is new and take seconds.

Read-only, always: the tracker only reads public data. It never asks you to sign a transaction and can never move funds.

      ❓ What am I looking at?
      Portfolio value
      Everything the wallet holds, in USD right now: its ETH plus every token with a live price on Robinhood Chain (from Dexscreener, or from the token's own pool). USD at today's ETH price.
      Value over time
      Token holdings valued at each pool's own on-chain price, USD at today's ETH price; the ETH balance is not included. A rise or fall is a change in value, not a return — buys, sells and transfers move it too.
      Token transactions
      Every transaction that moved a token to or from the wallet, whoever sent it. Plain ETH transfers and contract calls that move no token leave no log on the chain and are not listed.
      Account
      What the chain says the address is: a wallet, a wallet that delegated to smart-account code (EIP-7702), or a contract.
      ETH balance
      The wallet's native ETH — what it uses to pay gas and buy tokens.
      Unrealized PNL
      Paper profit or loss on tokens still held: today's value minus what the wallet paid for them (average cost).
      Realized PNL
      Profit or loss already locked in by selling: sale proceeds minus the average cost of what was sold.
      Average entry (cost basis)
      The average price paid per token in ETH across all buys. Tokens transferred in count as free, which pulls the average down.
      Held
      How many of that token the wallet holds right now, read from the chain.
      Hide dust
      Hides positions worth under $1 that were never traded, so the list stays readable.

      Trades are reconstructed by matching token transfers against known DEX pools; very deep histories are truncated and some legs can't be valued. USD figures use today's ETH price.
