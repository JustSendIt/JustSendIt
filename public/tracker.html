    ❓ What am I looking at?
    Portfolio value
    Everything the wallet holds, in USD right now: its ETH plus every token that has a live market on Robinhood Chain.
    ETH balance
    The wallet's native ETH — what it uses to pay gas and buy tokens.
    Unrealized PNL
    Paper profit or loss on tokens still held: today's value minus what the wallet paid for them (average cost).
    Realized PNL
    Profit or loss already locked in by selling: sale proceeds minus the average cost of what was sold.
    Average entry (cost basis)
    The average price paid per token in ETH across all buys. Tokens transferred in count as free, which pulls the average down.
    Held
    How many of that token the wallet holds right now, read from the chain.
    Hide dust
    Hides positions worth under $1 that were never traded, so the list stays readable.

    Trades are reconstructed by matching token transfers against known DEX pools; very deep histories are truncated and some legs can't be valued. USD figures use today's ETH price. This is an estimate for entertainment, not tax or investment advice.
